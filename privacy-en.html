Privacy Policy

App: What Today?

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. General Information

What Today? is an application for meal planning, managing grocery lists, recipes, shopping lists, and budgets.

We are committed to respecting our users' privacy.

2. Information Stored

The app stores information entered by users, including meal plans, product and shopping lists, expense records, budgets, and language preferences.

This information is stored locally in the user's browser on their device using localStorage. The app does not require registration and does not use a shared database for these entries.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share users' app entries with third parties. The website is hosted by Netlify. When users visit the website, Netlify may process technical information necessary to provide and secure its services, in accordance with its privacy policy.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

App entries are stored in the user's browser. Clearing browser data, deleting website data, or changing devices may result in loss of this information.

5. Children

The app is not specifically designed for children.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the app develops. The latest version will be available on this page.
